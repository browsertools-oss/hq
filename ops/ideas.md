# Idea pipeline

Ranked. Each idea: who pays and how, search demand checked (phrases and evidence), competition, time to first dollar, cost to the owner, score (1 to 10, expected profit per crew hour). The CEO keeps this current. Updated 11-21-2026.

## Gate for any launch
A new site with no links ranks for nothing for weeks, and the free-model request pool (about 1,000 a day) is already used by the two daily crews. Launch only when (a) one of the two sites shows that our pages can rank (a 30-day window above 300 visits) or AdSense approves, so a third site has a proven way to earn, and (b) request budget exists (lower AGENT_REQUESTS somewhere, or paid credits). Earliest likely: mid December 2026. Re-checked 11-21-2026: still no page from either site in `site:` searches; gate holds. Revisit when the owner decides on the paid cap after 11-27. Note 11-20-2026: the owner's paid-model test makes a crew several times faster (subfixer shipped 7 rows in one run), so crew time is no longer the limit if the owner keeps it; ranking still is.

## Ranked

1. **Fitness file tools (GPX, FIT, TCX): convert, merge, trim, view, fix timestamps.** Score 6 (guess).
   - Who pays: AdSense; later affiliate links to GPS watches and bike computers (needs an owner affiliate account); a small Pro for batch merge.
   - Demand checked 11-19-2026: "fit to gpx converter online" returns a mix of forum threads, a Windows how-to blog, OSM wiki pages, listoffreeware and small tools (dawarich.app, the5krunner, mygeodata, anyconv). No dominant big brand on page one. Volume not measured yet (head of growth to check).
   - Competition: gpx.studio and GPSVisualizer are strong for viewers and editors; plain converters are weak.
   - Fit: client-side parsing is easy (FIT SDK is open, GPX is XML). Fits the "files never leave your browser" brand and links naturally to nothing we have, so no cross-traffic.
   - Time to first dollar: 6 to 10 weeks after launch (indexing plus AdSense site add). Cost: $0 on .pages.dev.
   - Head of growth 11-20-2026: convert and merge are already served free and privately by strong incumbents; the openings are long-tail problems ("merge gpx files", "strava export fit.gz to gpx", "gpx to fit course for garmin"). Same guide play we are still testing. Holds behind the gate.
2. **Calendar file tools (CSV to ICS, ICS to CSV, ICS viewer, merge ICS).** Score 5 (guess). **Decided 11-20-2026: built inside stayfile, not a venture.** Head of growth (11-20): page one is small single-purpose tools and blog how-tos, no big brand; Apple Calendar has no CSV import (drives "csv to ics"); Google Calendar takes CSV directly, so "excel to google calendar" is a guide. Given to the stayfile boss for the 11-22 plan: 2 tools, 2 guides.
   - Who pays: AdSense; maybe a $5 one-time Pro (recurrence, many calendars). csvtoics.app already charges $7 for recurrence, which proves people pay.
   - Demand checked 11-19-2026: "csv to ics converter free online" page one is Softpedia, Taskade app pages, npm, a few small single-purpose sites (addcal, calget, devtoollab, icalconverter). Weak, but several small sites already compete.
   - Could live inside stayfile as a section instead of a new venture: cheaper, uses existing domain authority. Decide after the head of growth checks volume.
3. **Contacts file tools (CSV/Excel to VCF, VCF to CSV), a stayfile section, not a venture.** Score 5 (guess).
   - Why: the iPhone Contacts app has no CSV import, and Google Contacts exports CSV, so people moving contacts search "csv to vcf". Contacts are personal data, so "nothing is uploaded" is a real reason to pick us.
   - Demand checked 11-21-2026: page one for "csv to vcf converter online free import contacts iphone" is two small App Store apps, Wondershare MobileTrans, AnyConv, theonlineconverter, a SourceForge tool, Cisdem and SysTools how-tos, a Product Hunt page and DEV/Vocal posts. No dominant brand; several tools have a 100-contact limit or crash on big files. Volume not measured (head of growth to check).
   - Plan: after the calendar tools ship and pass `qa` (same column-mapping UI), 2 tools plus 1 guide ("import a CSV into iPhone contacts"). Given to the stayfile boss 11-21 as the next cluster.
4. **Subtitle and file guides for specific players and editors** (Plex, Jellyfin, Kodi, Premiere, DaVinci). Not a venture: given to the subfixer boss as direction on 11-19-2026.
